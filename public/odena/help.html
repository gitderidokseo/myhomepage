1. What is Odena?

If you drive for business, tax rules in most countries expect a proper mileage log — date, distance, purpose — before you can claim mileage reimbursement or deduct vehicle costs. Writing it by hand in a notebook or spreadsheet is easy to forget. Odena lets your phone write it for you.

The app does three things:

What it does
1 Detects when you start driving and starts recording automatically
2 Measures the distance with GPS while you drive
3 Exports a mileage log (Excel or PDF) when you need it for tax or expenses

There is no account and no sign-up. Your trips, vehicle and places are stored only on your phone. GPS is used only to calculate distance — the route you drove is not saved or sent anywhere. The app says so at the bottom of the trip screen.

2. Install the app

  1. Open Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone).
  2. Search for Odena — Odena: Mileage Tracker & Log, by LalaCatSoft.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.

💡 Odena is free. You don't enter any payment details.

3. First launch: country and your vehicle

Choose your country

The first screen asks which country you drive in. Your choice sets the distance unit (miles or kilometres), the mileage rate and the report format. Tap your country, then confirm.

💡 You can change it later in App settings, and you can also set a different country per vehicle (see Mileage rates).

Set up yourself and your vehicle

Odena starts right away — no company, no code. Fill in:

Field What to enter
Your name Shown as the driver's name on your reports
Licence plate (required) Your vehicle's registration number
Vehicle model Optional, e.g. Ford Focus — printed on the report
Current odometer reading (required) The total distance shown on your dashboard

Tap Start.

⚠️ Your trips are stored only on this phone. If you lose or replace it, they are gone — export your report (Excel/PDF) regularly to keep a copy.

Your vehicle card

Your vehicle now appears as a card with three buttons. These three are all you need:

Button When to tap it
⚙️ Vehicle link To turn on automatic trip recording, and to set the vehicle's country and mileage rate (chapter 6)
▶️ Trip screen To start driving and recording (chapter 4)
📋 Trip history To see, edit and export your trips (chapter 7)

The ⋮ menu on the card offers Settings (the same screen as Vehicle link), Edit (plate and model) and Delete. Deleting a vehicle keeps its trip history.

💡 Using Odena on your own (free) lets you register 1 vehicle.

The top bar of this screen has: Review trips (✅ icon, with a badge showing how many are waiting), Refresh, How to use (opens this guide) and App settings (⚙️).

4. Record your first trip

Tap Trip screen on your vehicle card.

① Choose the trip purpose

Tap Business, Commute or Personal.

  • Business — client visits, deliveries, work errands.
  • Commute — home ↔ regular workplace. How commutes are treated depends on your country's tax rules.
  • Personal — private trips. Kept in your log but left out of reimbursement.

You can change this afterwards at any time (chapter 7).

② Tap the green Start trip button

The first time, the phone asks for a few permissions. Please allow all of them — otherwise distance will be recorded wrongly. One by one:

(1) About location data collection → Agree

Odena explains that it uses GPS in the background, even when the screen is off, only to calculate distance. Tap Agree.

(2) Location permission → Precise + While using the app

  1. Choose Precise (the left of the two map pictures).
  2. Tap While using the app.

⚠️ Do not pick Approximate — distances will be wrong.

(3) Background location → Open settings → Allow all the time

Odena now explains why it needs location in the background. Tap Open settings.

  • Tap Allow all the time.
  • Make sure Use precise location is on (scroll down if you can't see it).
  • Go back with ←.

⚠️ This is the most important step. With While using the app only, distance measuring stops the moment you turn the screen off or switch to another app. You can't keep the screen on while driving — choose Allow all the time.

📱 iPhone: choose Allow While Using App first, then Change to Always Allow when iOS asks. Also keep Precise Location on (Settings → Odena → Location).

(4) Notifications → Allow (Android 13 and later)

While a trip is recording, Android shows the Recording trip notification. Tap Allow so it can be shown — it also keeps the recording running reliably in the background.

(5) Battery optimisation → Set up → Allow

  1. Tap Set up in Odena's dialog.
  2. Tap Allow in the system dialog (Let app always run in background?).

⚠️ If you refuse, the phone puts the app to sleep to save battery and distance goes missing mid-trip. (If you skip it now, Odena asks again next time.)

If Power saving mode is on, Odena warns you before starting — GPS is collected less often, so the distance may come out short. Turn power saving off for the trip, or tap Start anyway.

These permissions appear only the first time. After that, one tap on Start trip is all it takes.

③ While the trip is recording

  • The big number = distance of this trip.
  • The circle = time driven.
  • Total distance = the current odometer figure.
  • The notification Recording trip at the top means everything is working.

You can now put the phone down and drive. Turn the screen off, use other apps, even close Odena — distance keeps adding up.

💡 If you see Weak GPS signal · Distance for this section will be added once it recovers, you are probably in a tunnel or underpass. It is not a fault — the missing distance is filled in when the signal returns.

5. When the trip ends

Tap the red End trip button.

The Trip complete window shows the distance of this trip.

Write a visit purpose (optional)

Fill in Visit purpose — the place visited, work details, etc. (e.g. Client A — delivery). It appears in the Notes column of your report. In Germany, a business partner can be added as well (chapter 12).

💡 Save as the visit purpose for this location

Tick this and Odena remembers the place. Next time a trip ends nearby, the visit purpose is filled in for you.

  • Radius (m) — how close counts as "the same place". The default is fine for most places; reduce it if buildings are close together.
  • Save your regular destinations once and you will rarely need to type anything again — it also works for automatically ended trips.
  • To forget a place, finish a trip there, untick the box and save.

Tap Save to complete the record. (More about named places in chapter 9.)

6. Make trips record automatically (most important)

So far you pressed Start trip and End trip yourself. If you forget once, the log has a gap.

Let Odena start when you get in the car and stop when you get out — this is the real reason to use it.

Tap ⚙️ Vehicle link on the vehicle card.

① Turn on Use auto trip start

The Start method list appears. Choose one.

② Which method should I choose?

Bluetooth Beacon (BLE) Charging + GPS speed
What you need Nothing — just your car's Bluetooth A small beacon or USB BLE dongle (a few pounds/euros) Nothing — a charging cable in the car
Setup Very easy Buy it and plug it in Very easy
Android ✅ Works well ✅ Works well ✅ Works
iPhone ⚠️ Limited (see below) ✅ Recommended ✅ Works
Phone calls ⚠️ Call audio plays through the car speakers Quiet Quiet
Several vehicles ✅ One device per vehicle ✅ One beacon per vehicle ⚠️ One vehicle only

⚠️ Bluetooth and phone calls. The Bluetooth method needs your phone connected to the car stereo. When someone calls, the call comes out of the car speakers — awkward with passengers. A beacon doesn't need the phone connected to the stereo.

⚠️ iPhone and Bluetooth. iOS strongly restricts what apps can do with Bluetooth, so iPhone users should use a beacon.

In short:

  • Android, no purchase → Bluetooth (Method A)
  • iPhone, or you don't want call audio in the speakers → Beacon (BLE) (Method B)
  • No Bluetooth, no beacon, one car → Charging + GPS speed (Method C)

Method A. Bluetooth

When you start the engine, your phone connects to the car's Bluetooth. Odena uses that connection as the signal.

⚠️ Do this in the car, engine on, with the phone connected to the car's Bluetooth.

  1. Tap Link on the Bluetooth row.

  1. Allow the nearby devices permission.

  2. A list of paired Bluetooth devices appears. Select your car's stereo (often shown as the car's make or model).

⚠️ Don't pick wireless earbuds or a headset. Odena would start a trip every time you put your earbuds in.

  1. A ✓ and the device name appear. Done.

How it behaves:

  • 🚗 Engine on → phone connects → trip starts automatically
  • 🅿️ Engine off → Bluetooth disconnects → trip ends automatically after about 45 seconds

💡 The 45 seconds protect against traffic lights, tunnels and short drops. If the connection returns within that time, the trip simply continues.

💡 To change or remove the device, tap its name again (Tap to change or unlink).

📱 iPhone: iOS only lists the device that is connected right now. Connect to the car, then tap Refresh so the car appears. Because of this limit, iPhone users are better off with Method B.

Method B. Beacon (BLE)

A small transmitter (beacon) stays in the car; Odena detects it to know you are in the vehicle. Recommended for iPhone users and for anyone who doesn't want call audio on the speakers.

Get a beacon first

Prepare ① a USB car charger (cigarette-lighter socket — not needed if your car has a USB port) and ② a USB BLE dongle or beacon.

Phone What to look for
iPhone A product that explicitly says iBeacon (Minew and RECO are common brands)
Android Almost any BLE beacon or USB BLE dongle

⚠️ An iPhone can only see iBeacon-standard beacons. Cheap USB Bluetooth dongles may show up on Android but not on iPhone. Check the listing says "iBeacon", or ask the seller.

💡 Power. Many USB dongles switch off with the engine. That's fine — the signal disappearing is the "you got out" signal, so the trip ends. Battery-powered beacons (coin cell) can simply stay in the car.

Register it in the app

Plug it in, then do this inside the car.

  1. Tap Link (Android) or Register (iPhone) on the Beacon (BLE) row.

  2. Allow Physical activity (Android). Odena uses it to tell walking from riding in a car.

  1. After scanning, nearby BLE devices are listed. The stronger the signal (the number closer to 0, e.g. −44 vs −61), the closer the device. The top one is usually the dongle in your car.
  2. Tap it to link.

💡 Not sure which one is yours? Scan once with the dongle unplugged and once plugged in — the address that only appears when it's plugged in is yours.

💡 A Tesla advertises BLE by itself. It shows up in the list with no extra device.

📱 iPhone: Register beacon

  • Nearby beacons are listed with the brand (e.g. Minew), Major / Minor values and a rough distance (Very near · approx. 0.3 m).
  • Stand next to the beacon and pick the nearest one, then tap Save.
  • Nothing listed? Wait about 30 seconds near the beacon — beacons transmit only every few seconds — then tap Scan again. If it still doesn't appear, the product is probably not an iBeacon.
  • Enter manually (advanced) lets you type the beacon's UUID, Major and Minor.
  • iPhone needs Precise Location on to see beacons (Settings → Odena → Location → Precise Location).

Method C. Charging + GPS speed

The last alternative for people who use neither Bluetooth nor a beacon. You only need to plug your phone into a charger in the car.

Phone Where to turn it on Name
Android App settings → Auto trip detection Charging-detection auto trips
iPhone Vehicle link screen (scroll down) Charging + GPS speed
  • After you plug in and actually start driving, the trip starts automatically (iPhone: 5 km/h for 3 seconds).
  • Charging at home does not start a trip — you aren't moving.
  • Unplug and stay stopped, and the trip ends.
  • ⚠️ It cannot tell which vehicle it is, so only one vehicle is the target. Turning it on for another vehicle moves the target. With several vehicles, use Bluetooth or a beacon.

Vehicle manufacturer link

At the bottom of the vehicle settings. Optional. Linking a manufacturer account lets the app read the real odometer for more accurate distances. It does not change how trips start or end.

7. Your trip log: review, edit, add

Review automatic trips

Automatically recorded trips wait in a review inbox: tap the ✅ Review trips icon at the top of the vehicle list (a badge shows how many are waiting).

  • Each trip shows a suggested purpose based on your work schedule or on a saved place (Suggested by your schedule: Business, Suggested for 'Client A': Business).
  • A suggestion only applies when you confirm it. Tap Confirm as Business (etc.) per trip, or Confirm all for the lot.
  • This trip started outside your working hours tells you why a trip is suggested as personal.

Trip history

Tap Trip history on the vehicle card.

Each card shows the date and time, distance, duration, a purpose tag (Business / Commute / Personal), an Auto tag for automatically recorded trips, and your notes.

The top bar has ＋ Add a trip and ⤓ Export (see chapter 11). The Start trip button at the bottom right starts a trip from here.

Edit a trip

Tap a finished trip.

You can change the purpose, notes and addresses, and — for trips kept only on your phone — the distance. Below the form, Edit history lists every change.

💡 Changes are kept in an edit log and may appear in your report's appendix. That is deliberate: an unchangeable record of corrections makes your log more credible. 💡 A trip that is still recording can't be edited here — use the trip screen. 💡 Personal trips don't keep addresses.

Add a trip the app missed

Tap ＋ Add a trip in Trip history.

Enter date, start and end time, distance, purpose and notes. Odometer readings are optional. When you added the trip is recorded in the edit log, and added trips are marked in the report (Manually added).

Delete a trip

Open the trip and tap the 🗑 Delete trip icon at the top right.

This removes the trip and its edit history and can't be undone.

⚠️ In Germany and for Polish VAT 100% vehicles, trips can't be deleted (the log must stay complete). For a wrongly recorded trip, set the distance to 0 and write the reason in the notes.

Odometer

The odometer you entered at the start is the baseline. Odena adds each trip's distance to it. About every few months it asks you to check the dashboard again (Recheck odometer reading) — you can Skip, but matching it to the real odometer keeps the log accurate. If your entry differs a lot from the saved value, Odena asks you to double-check it.

To correct it any time, tap Edit total distance on the trip screen. An adjustment appears in the history as an Odometer adjustment card.

8. Work schedule

Sort automatic trips into business and personal by your working hours.

App settings → Work schedule

  1. Turn on Classify by work schedule.
  2. For each day, Add working hours (start/end; overnight shifts supported) or leave the day Off. Fill with Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00 gives you a quick start.
  3. Choose what happens to automatic trips outside working hours: - Record as personal — keeps the distance but leaves it out of reimbursement. - Don't record — such trips aren't saved at all (you can still tap Start trip yourself).

💡 The schedule applies only to automatic trips. Trips you start yourself keep the purpose you choose.

9. Frequent places

Give names to places such as your office or clients, so logs show Client A instead of a street address.

App settings → Frequent places

  • Tap Add current location while you are there, give it a name and radius.
  • Any trip starting or ending within the radius uses the place name.
  • When you save a place, you can apply it to earlier trips within its radius (kept in the edit log).
  • Usual purpose — when you review automatic trips that end here, this purpose is suggested.
  • In Germany, a business partner can be stored with the place.
  • You can also save a trip's destination as a place directly from Edit trip (Save destination as a place).
  • Personal trips don't keep addresses.

10. Mileage rates

If you claim mileage reimbursement, set the rate per vehicle.

Vehicle link → Mileage rate

Rate type Use it when
Standard rate You use your country's official rate table (e.g. HMRC in the UK). Odena shows the current table, the date it applies from and the source
Custom rate Your employer sets the rate. Add one or more rates with start and end dates
No reimbursement You only need the log; reports won't show amounts

Choose the Vehicle type (car or van / motorcycle / bicycle) where available. Odena warns you if a custom rate has expired or doesn't cover today.

The same vehicle settings screen has Vehicle country — it applies to future trips; past trips keep their country.

11. Export your report (Excel / PDF)

For tax returns, expense claims or your accountant.

Tap the ⤓ icon at the top right of Trip history.

  1. Choose the period (From / To month).
  2. Tap Download Excel file or Download PDF file.
  3. A share sheet opens — save it to Downloads or Drive, or send it by email or message.

The report contains a cover page, a summary (total, business and personal distance, business share, reimbursement), one row per trip (date, times, from/to, purpose, notes, odometer, distance, rate, amount) and the rate source. Distances are rounded to one decimal; amounts are calculated from the distances shown. Personal trips appear without addresses.

If odometer values are missing, Odena warns you first (Odometer distance is blank) — you can fill them in and export again, or Export anyway. If your name is missing for a country that wants it on the cover, you're asked as well.

💡 Export regularly. Your data lives only on your phone.

12. Country notes: UK, Poland, Germany

Odena adapts to your country. These forms follow general requirements and have not been reviewed by a tax adviser — check with yours before submitting.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

Distances are in miles. The Standard rate uses the HMRC approved mileage rates. Reports use the general mileage-log format.

🇵🇱 Poland

Distances are in kilometres. Reports can be exported in Polish in the art. 86a format.

  • Each settlement period (monthly or quarterly) must be confirmed: Confirm period → enter the odometer reading at the period end → Confirm. A reminder appears when a period is waiting. Later changes to a confirmed period are logged as changed after confirmation.
  • VAT 100% vehicle — for cars registered business-only (VAT-26). Turn it on in the vehicle's settings, choose the settlement period, and enter the log start date and odometer. ⚠️ Any personal trip is flagged, because recorded private use can cost you the 100% deduction.

🇩🇪 Germany

Distances are in kilometres. The Fahrtenbuch is always in German.

  • Every business trip needs a purpose, a business partner and a destination, and every trip needs odometer readings. Odena lists what needs attention (missing purpose, partner, destination or odometer, odometer gaps, late edits).
  • Fill it in promptly — edits made more than 24 hours after a trip are marked as late.
  • Trips can't be deleted (see chapter 7).

13. App settings

Vehicle list → ⚙️ at the top right → App settings. You don't have to change anything — look when you need to.

  • Usage mode — Phone storage (your data stays on your phone).
  • Your name — shown on report covers.
  • Country — distance unit, rates, report format. Also settable per vehicle.
  • Work schedule and Frequent places — see chapters 8 and 9.
  • Auto trip detection — one switch per method:
  • Detect boarding to start trips automatically — uses the phone's activity recognition (walking vs. in a vehicle).
  • Bluetooth (BT) auto trips
  • BLE auto trips (USB dongle)
  • Charging-detection auto trips

💡 Normally leave these alone — registering a device on the vehicle's Vehicle link screen turns the right switch on. Use this screen to pause automatic recording for a while. Which device a vehicle uses is chosen on its own Vehicle link screen.

  • Leave a review, Open-source licenses, Share debug log (creates a diagnostic file you can send to support).
  • Account & data → Delete all data on this phone — removes all trips, vehicles, places and settings. It can't be undone — export your reports first.

14. FAQ

Q. Does it cost anything? A. No. Odena is free, with no account and no sign-up.

Q. Where is my data? A. Only on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server. GPS is used only to calculate distance; the route and locations you drove are not stored or transmitted.

Q. Do I need to keep the screen on while driving? A. No. Recording continues with the screen off, with other apps in use, or with Odena closed — provided location is set to Allow all the time and battery optimisation is allowed (chapter 4).

Q. Does it drain my battery? A. GPS is used only while a trip is recording. The Beacon method scans in the background to find your car (in a low-power mode when you're not driving). The Bluetooth method only receives the connection signal from the phone, so its battery impact is negligible.

Q. What happens if I change or lose my phone? A. Trips live only on the phone, so export your report before changing phones and regularly in general.

Q. I have more than one vehicle. A. The free version registers 1 vehicle. (Charging + GPS speed also supports only one vehicle.)

Q. How long should I keep my mileage log? A. Check your tax authority's rules; many countries require several years. Keep your exported files safe, organised by year.

Q. Does it work on iPhone? A. Yes. Permission steps look slightly different, and a beacon is the recommended automatic method. iPhone differences are marked 📱 in this guide.

Q. When I'm connected to the car by Bluetooth, my calls come out of the car speakers. A. Yes — that's how the Bluetooth method works: the phone must stay connected to the car stereo. If that bothers you, switch to Beacon (BLE).

Q. Where do I buy a beacon, and how much is it? A. Online marketplaces and electronics shops, usually a few pounds/euros. For iPhone, search for iBeacon and check the listing says iBeacon; for Android, a BLE beacon or USB BLE dongle works. Add a USB car charger if your car has no USB port.

Q. Is Odena a tax adviser? A. No. It produces a log in the format commonly required, but tax rules differ by country and by situation. Check with your tax adviser — especially for the Polish and German forms.

15. Troubleshooting

🔴 Distance is too short / shows 0

Usually a permission issue. Check, in order:

  1. Location = Allow all the time? Settings → Apps → Odena → Permissions → Location → Allow all the time, and Use precise location on. (iPhone: Settings → Odena → Location → Always, Precise Location on.)
  2. Battery optimisation allowed? Settings → Battery → Odena → Unrestricted / Don't optimise (names differ by manufacturer).
  3. Power saving mode off? It reduces GPS collection. Odena warns you at the start.
  4. Did the trip start in an underground car park? No GPS there; the distance until you reach the surface can be missed.
  5. Still wrong? App settings → Share debug log and send it to support.

🔴 I got in the car but the trip didn't start automatically

  1. Is Use auto trip start on in the vehicle's Vehicle link screen?
  2. Is a device actually registered (a ✓ next to it)?
  3. Is the phone's Bluetooth on and connected to the car?
  4. Did you pick the car stereo and not earbuds?
  5. In App settings → Auto trip detection, is the switch for your method on?
  6. If you set Work schedule → Don't record, trips outside working hours aren't started — Odena says so and you can tap Start trip yourself.

🔴 A trip starts when I'm not in the car

You probably linked earbuds or a speaker over Bluetooth. Go to Settings → Bluetooth → tap the device name and pick again.

🔴 📱 iPhone: my beacon doesn't appear

  1. Wait ~30 seconds next to it.
  2. Is the dongle powered? Many USB types only power up with the engine on (usually a small LED blinks).
  3. Is Precise Location on? Settings → Odena → Location → Precise Location.
  4. Still nothing → probably not an iBeacon product. Ask the seller, and exchange or return it.

🔴 The trip doesn't end automatically

  • It ends about 45 seconds after the signal drops — wait a moment.
  • A trip ends only by the method that started it. A trip you started by hand must be ended by hand.
  • To end it now: Trip screen → End trip.

🔴 "Weak GPS signal" keeps showing

Normal in tunnels, underpasses and between tall buildings. Not a fault — the missing section is added when the signal returns.

🔴 The odometer doesn't match my dashboard

Trip screen → Edit total distance and enter the real reading. GPS distance is normally very slightly shorter than the real distance, so adjust it now and then.

🔴 The report says Odometer distance is blank

The first trip in the period has no odometer value. Enter your odometer reading (Edit total distance); earlier trips are filled in automatically. Then export again.

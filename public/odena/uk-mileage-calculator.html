Things to know

  • This calculator is for sole traders and partnerships using simplified expenses. Employees claiming for their own car follow different rules (approved mileage allowance payments).
  • You cannot use flat rates for a vehicle you have already claimed capital allowances on, and once you use them for a vehicle you must keep using them for it.
  • Parking and tolls can be claimed on top of the flat rate. Fuel, insurance and repairs are already covered by it.
  • The saving shown is income tax only. Class 4 National Insurance on your profits may reduce too.

To claim, you need a mileage log

HMRC can ask you to show how you arrived at your business mileage. Keep a record of each journey: the date, start and end points, the reason for the trip and the miles. Writing this up at the end of the year from memory is where claims go wrong.

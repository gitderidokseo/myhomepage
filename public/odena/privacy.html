Odena Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 October 2026

In short. Odena keeps your trips, vehicles and places on your phone. There is no account and we do not receive your trip records. A few things do leave your phone — address look-ups by your phone's operating system, and small requests for mileage rate tables and app notices that carry only your country and language. 1. Who we are

Odena is a mileage logbook app made by LalaCatSoft (Republic of Korea), referred to as "we" or "us". This policy covers the Odena app for Android and iOS (package / bundle ID app.odena.logbook) used without an account ("solo" use). For anything that leaves your phone as described in section 3, LalaCatSoft is the controller.

Contact: support@lalacatsoft.com

2. Data stored on your phone

The following is created by you or by the app and is stored only in the app's storage on your phone. We cannot see it.

Data Details
Your name Printed on the reports you export.
Vehicles Number plate, model, owner name, odometer readings, country, mileage rate and tax settings, and the name/address of the car's Bluetooth device or beacon if you link one.
Trips Start and end time, start and end location (coordinates and address), distance, purpose (business / personal / commute), notes, customer or business partner, odometer before and after, and whether the trip was recorded automatically. The app adds up distance while you drive but does not save your full route.
Personal trips For the United Kingdom, Poland and Germany, once a trip is marked personal its coordinates and addresses are erased and only the time and distance are kept.
Places Places you save (name, location, address, default purpose, customer).
Edit history Changes you make to trips after they are recorded (what changed, old and new values, when). Some tax rules require this history.
Settings Country, working hours, period confirmations, cached rate tables.
Diagnostic log A technical log on your phone that includes GPS readings (location, accuracy, speed) taken while recording, used to fix recording problems. It is limited in size and only leaves your phone if you choose "Share debug log" in settings.
Exported reports Excel and PDF reports you create. A copy is kept in the app's storage on your phone.

3. Data that leaves your phone

3.1 Address look-up (your phone's operating system)

To show addresses instead of coordinates, Odena asks your phone's built-in geocoding service to turn the start and end coordinates of your trips (and places you add at your current location) into addresses. On Android this service is provided by Google, on iOS by Apple, and they receive the coordinates under their own privacy policies (Google, Apple). Personal trips are not looked up. We do not receive the coordinates or the addresses.

3.2 Mileage rate tables and app notices (our servers)

When the app starts, it downloads the current mileage rate table for your country, and once per session it checks for app notices. These requests contain only your country code and app language — no name, no device ID, no trip data. Our servers run on Google Cloud (Firebase) in the United States. As with any internet request, your IP address and basic technical data (such as time of request) reach the server and may be kept in the hosting provider's standard request logs for up to 30 days for security and troubleshooting. We do not use them to identify you.

3.3 What you choose to share

  • Reports and logs — when you export a report or share the diagnostic log, it goes to the app or person you pick in your phone's share menu. From then on, the recipient's privacy terms apply.
  • Ratings — the app may show the Google Play or App Store rating prompt. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google or Apple.
  • Links — help and policy links open our website in your browser.

3.4 Phone backups

Your phone's own backup (Android backup to your Google account, or iCloud / computer backup on iOS) may include Odena's data. These backups are controlled by you and your phone's settings and are stored by Google or Apple; we cannot access them.

3.5 What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or e-mail address is needed.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.

4. Permissions and why we ask

Permission Why
Location (while using the app) To measure distance and record where a trip starts and ends.
Location in the background ("Allow all the time" / "Always") To keep recording when the screen is off and to record trips automatically when you start driving. Location is used only while a trip is being recorded or checked.
Physical activity / Motion To notice that you are in a vehicle and start or stop automatic trips.
Bluetooth / Nearby devices To start and stop trips when your phone connects to your car or detects your car's beacon. Not used to find your location.
Notifications To show the "recording" notification while a trip is in progress.
Ignore battery optimisation So the system does not stop recording in the middle of a trip.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. Automatic recording will then not work, but you can still add trips by hand.

5. Legal bases (EU and UK GDPR)

  • Providing the app you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)) — address look-ups, rate tables and notices that make the app work.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping our servers secure and working through short-lived request logs.

6. International transfers

LalaCatSoft is in the Republic of Korea, which the European Commission and the United Kingdom recognise as providing adequate protection. Our servers are operated by Google in the United States; Google participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK extension) and also offers Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it. Tax rules may require you to keep your logbook for several years — export your reports before deleting.
  • Erase everything: Settings → Account & data → Delete all data on this phone deletes trips, vehicles, places, edit history, settings, the diagnostic log and the report copies stored by the app, and turns off automatic recording.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes its data from the phone (but not from backups you made — see 3.4).
  • Server request logs are deleted automatically after up to 30 days.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to receive it in a portable format. Because your logbook is stored only on your phone, you can do most of this yourself in the app (edit, export, erase). For anything else, write to support@lalacatsoft.com.

You can also complain to a data protection authority — for example the ICO (UK), UODO (Poland), or your German federal state's data protection authority.

9. If you share reports with an employer

If you send a report to your employer, accountant or tax office, they decide how to use it and their own privacy terms apply. Odena does not send anything to them by itself.

10. Children

Odena is meant for adult drivers and is not directed at children under 16.

11. Security

Your logbook is protected by your phone's own security (screen lock and app sandbox). Requests to our servers use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.

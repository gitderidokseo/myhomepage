Mileage logbook

Odena

Odena records your trips automatically and turns them into a mileage logbook for the tax office — HMRC mileage in the UK, ewidencja przebiegu pojazdu in Poland and the Fahrtenbuch in Germany. Your logbook stays on your phone.

Getting started

  1. Choose your country and add your vehicle with its current odometer reading.
  2. Allow location "all the time" so trips are recorded with the screen off.
  3. Optionally link your car's Bluetooth or a beacon so trips start and stop by themselves.
  4. Review trips, mark them business or personal, and export Excel or PDF reports.

Your data

There is no account. Trips, vehicles and places are stored only on your phone — export your reports regularly, because we cannot restore them for you. To delete everything, use Settings → Account & data → Delete all data on this phone. Details are in the privacy policy.

Support

Questions or problems: support@lalacatsoft.com. If a trip was not recorded correctly, Settings → Share debug log and attach the file to your e-mail (the log contains location readings — share it only with us).
